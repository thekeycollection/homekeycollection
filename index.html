Before you send your room

How to measure your room.

Accurate measurements are how we make sure every piece actually fits. It takes about 10 minutes, and you don't need to be exact. Rounding to the nearest inch is perfect.

What to measure · bird's-eye view
Tape measureYour Notes app (or paper & pencil)A helper, if you have one
  1. A

    Measure every wall

    Measure each wall from corner to corner along the floor (A and B on the diagram). Start at the door and work your way around, noting which wall is which.

  2. C

    Measure the windows

    Note each window's width and height, how far it sits from the nearest corner, and the height from the floor to the bottom of the sill.

  3. D

    Measure the doors

    Note each doorway's width, its distance from the nearest corner, and which way the door swings, so nothing blocks it.

  4. E

    Measure the ceiling height

    Measure from the floor to the ceiling. This helps us choose the right size lighting, art, and tall pieces.

  5. F

    Mark the fixed features

    Note any outlets, vents, radiators, light fixtures, a fireplace, or built-ins, with their distance from the nearest corner.

  6. +

    Measure what you're keeping

    For any furniture staying in the room, note its width, depth, and height.

Photo tips

  • Take one photo of each wall, plus one from each corner.
  • Shoot during the day with the lights on and the curtains open.
  • Hold your phone horizontally and stand as far back as you can.
  • Send your measurements along with your photos so we can match each number to the right wall.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to take the Style Key quiz first?

No, but it helps. Your quiz result gives us a head start on your taste, so your redesigned room feels like you from the first draft.

What do I need to send?

A clear photo of your room (or a few), the room's measurements, your budget, and any pieces you're keeping. The questionnaire walks you through all of it.

Where are the pieces from?

We shop a mix of trusted retailers such as Wayfair, Target, and Amazon. Every item comes with a link.

How long does it take?

You'll get your redesigned room within 7 business days of sending your photos and questionnaire.

Can I request changes?

Each Room Key includes one round of small swaps.

Does it include installation or furniture?

No. The Room Key is a design plan with shopping links. You buy the pieces directly from the retailers.
